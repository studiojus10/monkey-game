# Infinite Monkeys

An idle game about infinite monkeys typing at random until they write Shakespeare. It runs in your terminal and is written in TypeScript. The full design and build plan are in [`docs/DESIGN.md`](docs/DESIGN.md).

## Playing

Requires Node 22.12 or newer.

```bash
npm install
npm start
```

The home screen has a title bar with your four currencies (Bananas, Words, Sentences, Lines, each in its own colour), a table of your monkeys (speed, energy, combo and what each is typing), the alphabet with the letters you own in their places, your script progress, a LATELY feed of what just happened, and your pinned lines. The everyday keys sit at the bottom; `?` lists every key.

## What's in it

- **A monkey** at a typewriter, typing random letters from the letters you own. It starts slow, one letter every 2 seconds.
- **Your first letter**: A or I, since it has to be a word on its own. Every word the monkey types scores (sum of Scrabble-style letter values) × length, so `I` is 1, `IT` is 4 and `ZAP` is 42. Everything shows in capitals; the live run of words is bold.
- **Combos**: words typed back to back form a chain. Each different word of 4+ letters in it raises the chain's multiplier by one, up to ×3. Sentences raise the cap. A letter that fits no word breaks the chain. A chain of 3+ words of 4+ letters pays a **Sentence** when it breaks, plus one more for every 3 further long words. Each monkey's current multiplier shows next to its line, and the live part of the line is bold.
- **More letters**, bought with Bananas, so longer words become possible. Words come from the public-domain ENABLE list in `data/words.txt`, plus A and I.
- **The script**: you start by opening a department for comedies, histories or tragedies and taking one short scene free. More scenes, acts (20% off) and whole plays (40% off) cost Bananas. Plays: A Midsummer Night's Dream, Richard III, Hamlet, Macbeth. When a monkey types one of its lines word for word (spaces and punctuation don't matter), the line is written into your script. The first time pays a **Line** and a Bananas bonus that grows steeply with the line's length; lines of 3+ words also pay a **Sentence** every time. Texts are the public-domain Moby Shakespeare via [the MIT Shakespeare](https://github.com/TheMITTech/shakespeare), converted with `scripts/import-play.ts`.
- **Milestones**: writing every line of a scene gives a permanent Bananas bonus (bigger for longer scenes) and some Sentences; finishing an act gives a permanent typing speed bonus (bigger for longer acts) and a free monkey. Bonuses add up the same however you bought the scenes.
- **Departments**: one per genre. Each holds up to 10 typing monkeys plus a manager, and has its own word slots; you pick each new monkey's department. The second opens at 10 monkeys and the third at 20; each monkey only writes plays of its department's genre. A fourth, the **Sonnets**, opens last at 30: verse lines pay 2 Lines each, and a finished sonnet pays a Line per line in it.
- **Office space**: you start in a room of 4 squares. Each monkey gets a free desk on a square of its department's room, so floor space limits how many monkeys a department can have. Grow rooms one square at a time, give each new department its own room, and buy break rooms for furniture. Everything places itself, but the office map (`o`) lets you design the layout: choose where rooms grow and where furniture, desks and windows stand. Inspect your furniture: something is hidden in the office, and a well-arranged office can make monkeys a little happier.
- **Energy**: monkeys tire as they type. A tired monkey is slower, down to 75% of its speed when worn out, and then takes a one-minute break on its own and comes back rested. Each monkey's speed right now and its energy show in its row: green above 80%, yellow above 20%, red below that, and a blue Resting on a break. Furniture helps the department whose room or lounge it's in: decor slows tiring, comforts speed up breaks, and every piece adds happiness: a Bananas bonus, and happy monkeys tire slower and rest faster. Petting adds a little happiness to one monkey, which wears off.
- **Hats**: dress up individual monkeys with eight hats, from a 25-Sentence baseball cap to a billion-Sentence halo, each giving that monkey a small boost, and dye them in six colours.
- **More monkeys**, bought with Bananas. Each types its own line and can be named. The first 10 are shown in a table under the title bar.
- **Typewriter keys**, once you own 13 letters: combination keys (`TH`, `ING`, `QU`, ...) and word keys type their whole text in one keystroke. A word of 3+ letters becomes a word key once your monkeys have spelled it 100 times (10% more for each word key you own). Only useful keys are offered; a test showed junk clusters dilute the pool, while good keys roughly double Bananas per keystroke.
- **Word slots**, once you own 13 letters, bought with Lines: each locks one position in a chain to a word you choose ("3rd word: FRIEND"), typed as a finished word. Switch slots off any time for fully random typing. Pinned lines show slot words in bold where they match.
- **Faster typing**, bought with Bananas for each monkey separately: each level of a monkey's typing skill makes it type 25% faster and costs more than the last. Its typewriter caps the skill (10 levels on the Rusty typewriter); a better typewriter allows 10 more.
- **Autosave** to `save.json` every 10 seconds and on quit. Set `IDLE_SAVE=path/to/file.json` to use a different file, which is handy for testing from a fresh start.
- **Offline progress**: the monkeys keep typing while you're away, up to 8 hours.

## Development

```bash
npm test           # run unit tests
npm run typecheck  # type-check without running
```

Names and balance numbers are all in [`src/game/config.ts`](src/game/config.ts); the terminal screen picks changes up automatically.
